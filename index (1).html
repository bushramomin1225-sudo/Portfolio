About

A builder who starts with the user

I'm Bushra Momin, a Computer Science student who likes building things that solve a real, specific problem.

My projects started with small questions. How does a hostel owner keep verification records without piles of paper? How can a college test students fairly and give results instantly? How do you send a parent one useful idea a day, in their own language, on the app they already use?

I enjoy the whole path: understanding the people involved, designing the data model, writing the code and shipping it.
